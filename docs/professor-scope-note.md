Edupredict is a course prototype that estimates the risk of a final grade below 10 (0–20 scale) from the first-period grade, past failures and study time. It is an offline experiment, not a validated early-warning service.

**Proposed scope:** one dataset (UCI Student Performance, Mathematics, 395 records); an offline comparison of three simple models with rules fixed before training (`docs/evaluation-protocol.md`); a locally running website demo.

**Approved:** the broad topic.
**Not yet confirmed:** the reduced scope, whether a local demo is sufficient (or hosting is required), and the use of the Mathematics dataset.
